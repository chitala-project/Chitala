# Trademark policy

The code of Chitala OS is open under Apache-2.0: you may use, modify and distribute it, including commercially. **The name and the logo are not part of that grant.** This policy says how they may be used, so that anyone can tell the official Chitala apart from forks and products built on it.

This is the project's policy, not legal advice. It does not claim that any mark is registered.

## The marks

- the name **CHITALA** and **Chitala OS**, in any capitalisation;
- the **Chitala logo** (`docs/assets/chitala-logo.png`, `docs/assets/chitala-logo-512.png`);
- the designations **"Official Chitala"**, **"Chitala Compatible"** and **"Chitala Certified"** (see [`CERTIFICATION.md`](CERTIFICATION.md)), and the **"Chitala Specification"** (see [`SPECIFICATION_POLICY.md`](SPECIFICATION_POLICY.md)).

## Relation to the license

Section 6 of the Apache License 2.0 grants no permission to use the licensor's trade names, trademarks, service marks or product names, except as required for reasonable and customary use in describing the origin of the work and reproducing the content of the NOTICE file. This policy explains what that means for Chitala.

The logo files are in the repository so the project can show them and so others can refer to the project. They are not licensed for any other use, whatever the license of the surrounding files (see [`NOTICE`](NOTICE)).

## You may, without asking

- say truthfully that your work is **based on**, **derived from**, **a fork of**, **uses** or **works with** Chitala OS;
- redistribute **unmodified official releases** under the name Chitala OS;
- use the name and the unmodified logo to **link to or talk about** the project (articles, talks, documentation, comparisons);
- keep the copyright and NOTICE attributions the license requires.

## You may not, without written permission

- name a fork, product, service, company, domain or package **"Chitala"** or anything confusingly similar;
- call a modified version **"Chitala"**, **"Chitala OS"**, **"Official Chitala"** or an **official release**;
- say or suggest that the project **endorses** or **certifies** your work;
- use **"Chitala Compatible"** or **"Chitala Certified"** except as granted under [`CERTIFICATION.md`](CERTIFICATION.md);
- call a modified specification the **"Chitala Specification"**;
- use a **modified** logo, or the logo as part of your own logo or branding.

## Forks

Forks are welcome. A fork distributed to others should:

- use its own name;
- remove the Chitala logo from its branding and user interface;
- keep the license and NOTICE attributions;
- describe itself, if it wishes, as "based on Chitala OS".

## Asking for permission

Open an issue, or contact the Project Lead ([@traderviet](https://github.com/traderviet)) for anything that should not be public. Permission is given in writing and may be limited in scope and time.
